What is VLAN segmentation, and why shouldn't every device share one network?

Network Design & Architecture

Reel voiceover script
  1. What is VLAN segmentation, and why shouldn't every device share one network?
  2. On one flat network, a single infected device may be able to reach many other systems.
  3. A VLAN divides a physical network into separate logical networks. Access rules control traffic between them.
  4. Example zones: staff, guest Wi-Fi, servers, and CCTV and IoT devices.
  5. Is your network segmented? UB Shield can help plan it at ubshield.in/contact

Vulnerability Assessment vs. Penetration Testing: what's the difference?

VAPT

Reel voiceover script
  1. Vulnerability assessment versus penetration testing. What's the difference? Related, but not the same.
  2. A vulnerability assessment looks for known and possible weaknesses across the agreed systems, and rates their risk.
  3. Penetration testing safely attempts to exploit weaknesses, with permission, to confirm what an attacker could actually do.
  4. A simple example. Assessment: this door lock looks weak. Penetration test: with your permission, we checked whether it can be opened.
  5. Which do you need? It depends on your systems and goals. Ask us at ubshield.in/contact

Caption

One finds possible weaknesses. The other checks how they could be exploited. Together they give a clearer picture.
